Exvent eWind and eAir ventilation units, and units with EDA automation behind a Freeway WEB adapter, connect over Modbus TCP and show up as a single device with live temperatures, humidity, heat recovery efficiency, fan speed and filter status. Home, Away, Overpressure, Boost and Off can be switched by hand or from a Flow, along with target temperature and heating. eWind and eAir add eco mode and Enhanced ventilation; EDA units add overpressure as a quick action, season control for heating and cooling, and heat pump readings such as defrosting.

Before adding an eWind or eAir, enable Modbus TCP from its panel and give the unit a fixed IP address in your router. You then only need that IP address and the port, which is 502 on a standard installation. Before adding an EDA unit, enter your Homey's IP address as the Modbus/TCP client in the Freeway WEB adapter's web interface, and give both the adapter and Homey a fixed IP address.
